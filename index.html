Frequently asked questions

What services do you offer?

We help with laptops, desktops, software and operating systems, and we supply computers and IT products. If your problem is not listed, call or message us and we will tell you if we can help.

How much will it cost?

It depends on the device and the problem, so we give a quote instead of fixed prices. Call or send a WhatsApp message describing the problem and we will explain the likely fix and the cost before any work begins.

Do you visit homes and offices in Hyderabad?

Yes. We provide service across all of Hyderabad. Contact us with your location and the problem, and we will confirm the visit.

What are your working hours?

We are available from 10am to 9pm, including weekends. Call or message us and we will arrange a time.

How do I book a service?

Call +91 73963 45795 or message us on WhatsApp. Tell us your device, what is going wrong, and whether you prefer to bring it in or have us visit.

How long will a repair take?

It depends on the problem. We will give you an estimate when we understand the issue, and tell you if anything changes.

Should I back up my files before service?

Yes, if you can. Some fixes, such as reinstalling an operating system, can affect files on the device. Tell us about any important files when you contact us so we can plan around them.

Can you help my business buy computers?

Yes. We can help you choose and buy laptops, desktops and accessories that suit your office and budget. Ask us for a quote.
